# Analyzing-Gamification-in-E-learning-tools-in-BD
Analyzing Gamification in E-learning tools : HCI-Driven Insights from University Students in Bangladesh

Abstract
Gamification has emerged as a widely adopted strategy to enhance
learner motivation and engagement in online education, yet its
impact in South Asian contexts remains underexplored. This study
investigates how gamification features in e-learning platforms influence
learner engagement among university students in Bangladesh.
A survey of 218 students across multiple universities was conducted
to capture perceptions of gamified features and expectations of an
ideal learning system. The study identified seven key gamification
elements interactive tasks, lesson completion rewards, leaderboards,
achievement animations, task completion discounts, free
preview lessons, and detailed course outlines—and examined their
prevalence across 24 platforms commonly used in Bangladesh. A
within-subjects design ensured consistency, and statistical analysis,
including heatmap visualizations, revealed that top-ranked
platforms consistently integrated core features such as interactive
tasks, rewards, previews, and course outlines. In contrast, low and
mid-tier platforms lacked such features, relying instead on minimal
transparency mechanisms. Findings validate the hypothesis
that lack of gamification significantly reduces engagement, while
also highlighting mismatches between student expectations and
platform design. This study contributes to the literature by providing
context-specific, HCI-driven insights into gamification in
Bangladesh and offers evidence-based guidance for the design of
culturally relevant and student-centered e-learning systems.
